About the Department

Who We Are

The Punjab Probation & Parole Department (PPNPS) is a statutory body operating under the Government of Punjab. Established to manage and supervise offenders released on probation or parole, our mission is to reduce recidivism through structured rehabilitation.

We work with courts, prisons, and social welfare agencies to ensure each individual under our supervision receives the guidance, skills training, and moral support needed to reintegrate into society as a law-abiding citizen.

Our network of probation officers across all 36 districts of Punjab maintains regular contact with probationers, monitors compliance with court orders, and connects individuals with educational and vocational opportunities.

Our Mission

Three Pillars of Our Work

Supervision

Regular monitoring and reporting of probationers to ensure compliance with court-ordered conditions across all 36 districts of Punjab.

Education & Training

Connecting probationers with vocational skills, literacy programs, and employment opportunities to build a self-sufficient future.

Family Reintegration

Facilitating counselling and mediation services to rebuild family relationships and create stable home environments for successful reintegration.

What We Offer

Our Services

1

Probation Supervision

Assigned probation officers conduct regular home and office visits to monitor the rehabilitation progress of each probationer.

2

Pre-Sentence Investigation Reports

Preparation of detailed background reports submitted to courts to assist judges in determining appropriate sentences.

3

Counselling & Psychological Support

Professional mental health and behavioural counselling services for probationers and their families through our district offices.

4

Vocational & Skills Training

Coordination with TEVTA and other agencies to enrol probationers in trade and skills development courses.

5

Aftercare Services

Post-probation follow-up and social support to ensure long-term stability and prevent recidivism after case closure.

6

Court Liaison

Direct communication with judiciary for reporting compliance, requesting modifications, and submitting progress evaluations.
