## 📌 About

This project is a professional government-style web portal designed for the **Punjab Probation & Parole Department (PPNPS)** — a statutory body operating under the Government of Punjab, Pakistan.

The department supervises, counsels, and rehabilitates offenders placed on probation, helping them reintegrate into society through structured support programs across all **36 districts of Punjab**.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🏠 Hero section with department introduction and call-to-action
- 📊 Live stats — Districts Covered, Probation Officers, Cases Managed
- 👤 About the Department section
- 🏛️ Three Pillars — Supervision, Education & Training, Family Reintegration
- 🛠️ Six core services listed with descriptions
- 📬 Contact section with head office details and inquiry form
- 🔔 Notice ticker / announcement bar
- 🌐 Urdu language link support
- ♿ Accessibility-ready structure
- 📱 Fully responsive — mobile friendly
- 🎨 Official government portal design aesthetic

## 📋 Services Covered

1. **Probation Supervision** — Regular home and office visits by assigned officers
2. **Pre-Sentence Investigation Reports** — Background reports for courts
3. **Counselling & Psychological Support** — Mental health services for probationers
4. **Vocational & Skills Training** — Coordination with TEVTA for trade courses
5. **Aftercare Services** — Post-probation follow-up and social support
6. **Court Liaison** — Direct communication with judiciary for compliance reporting
